Research

Dynamics, stability
& control.

I work on the analysis and control of nonlinear dynamical systems at the Centre de Recherche en Automatique de Nancy (CRAN), Université de Lorraine.

01

Nonlinear dynamical systems

Understanding stability and the behaviour of nonlinear systems, with applications to control design.

02

Contraction theory

Studying how trajectories approach one another, and using incremental stability and contraction metrics in analysis and design.

03

Output regulation & integral action

Designing controllers for reference tracking, disturbance rejection and nonlinear integral action.

04

Multi-agent systems

Understanding synchronization and coordination in networks of interconnected dynamical systems.
