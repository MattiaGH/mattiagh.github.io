Curriculum vitae

A path through
systems & control.

From automation engineering in Bologna to nonlinear control in Lyon, Tel Aviv and Nancy.

Originally from Ancona, Italy, I received my bachelor’s and master’s degrees in Automation Engineering from the University of Bologna, both with honours. I completed my Ph.D. in Control Theory at LAGEPP, Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1, in 2022.

After a postdoctoral position at Tel Aviv University, I joined Université de Lorraine in September 2023 as an Associate Professor, working at CRAN and ENSEM.
